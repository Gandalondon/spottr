About

Ask Relay where to go, how to get out, or what's next on your quest. Tap the microphone and talk; it keeps listening for follow-ups. Everything runs on your phone, so it works without internet once the voice is downloaded.

Unofficial companion, not made or endorsed by Embark Studios. Map data from MetaForge (metaforge.app). Place names and distances from allthings.how. Voice: Supertonic 3 by Supertone (OpenRAIL-M licence), running on sherpa-onnx (Apache 2.0).
